# End-user guide

For the person connecting to the gateway with a 3270 emulator. You need three
things from your administrator: the **host and port** to connect to, your
**user ID**, and your **password**.

The screen samples in this guide are real captures from a live gateway (via
the s3270 emulator); your site's branding, services, and system ID will
differ.
