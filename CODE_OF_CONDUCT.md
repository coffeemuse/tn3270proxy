# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment
for everyone who participates, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standard

We adopt the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as the code of conduct for this
project. Please read the full text here:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

In short: be respectful and considerate, assume good faith, and help keep
discussion constructive. Conduct that harasses, demeans, or excludes others is not
welcome here.

## Enforcement

Concerns about someone's conduct may be reported privately to the maintainer at
**rj+tn3270proxy AT coffeemuse DOT dev**. All reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
comments that violate this code, and may bar anyone who behaves unacceptably from
further participation.
